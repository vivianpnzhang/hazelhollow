About HazelHollow

A note from the founder

Most budgeting apps assume you get paid every two weeks. Students don't. We get one big chunk of money at the start of the semester and hope it lasts.

I wanted something that works the way students actually live, so I built HazelHollow. It's named after the way squirrels gather hazelnuts in the fall and tuck them away to last the winter. You add what you have, and HazelHollow tells you how much you can spend each week, so you make it to the end of the semester feeling okay, not stressed.

It's meant to feel cozy, not strict. No guilt, no scary red numbers. Just a calm little place to keep your stash.

— Vivian, Founder

The story behind the name

Hazel is for hazelnuts, the little treasures a squirrel gathers every fall. A hollow is the cozy space inside a tree where it tucks them away, safe until spring.

That's what HazelHollow is for: a safe, warm place for your stash. Our squirrel lives in its own tree hollow inside the app, and the more you check in, the cozier its home gets: a little lamp, a rug, a tiny bookshelf, string lights.

Money for students comes in harvests, like OSAP, a summer job, or help from family. HazelHollow helps one harvest last until the next.

What HazelHollow is (and isn't)

  • It's a free budgeting tool, built by a student, for students and anyone else.
  • Your numbers stay on your own device. There are no accounts and no bank connections.
  • It's not a bank or a registered business, and it never holds, moves or lends money. It's not financial advice, either.
