Privacy policy

The short version: your budget never leaves your device. We only collect information when you choose to fill out one of our forms, like applying to the Founding Team.

HazelHollow is a student-built project run by Vivian Zhang in Ontario, Canada. This policy follows the principles of Canada's federal privacy law for businesses, PIPEDA (the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act).

1. The app: nothing leaves your device

  • Everything you type into the app (your money, bills, spending, goals, streaks) is saved only in your browser's storage on your own phone or computer.
  • We don't have accounts or logins, and we can't see your numbers. They are never sent to us or to any server.
  • We don't use advertising, tracking cookies or analytics tools.
  • We never ask for bank logins, card numbers or Social Insurance Numbers (SIN). Please never type them anywhere in HazelHollow.
  • If you import a bank statement file (a Pro feature), it is read on your device only and is never uploaded.

2. Your controls

  • Export: in the app, go to Me → Your data → Export my data to download everything as a file.
  • Delete: Me → Your data → Delete all my data erases everything from your device. Clearing your browser's site data does the same.

3. Our website

When you visit the site, your device downloads the pages and small data files (like the team list and weekly missions). Like almost every website, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (for example IP addresses and the pages requested) to keep the site running and secure. We don't use these to identify you.

4. Forms you choose to fill out

The Founding Team application asks for your name, school and program, year, email, LinkedIn link (optional) and which role you want. The feedback form asks about your experience with the app. We use this information to:

  • review applications and send you your team code and weekly missions,
  • improve HazelHollow based on your feedback,
  • list you on the Founding Team wall, only if you say yes, and
  • write a LinkedIn recommendation, if you earn one and want one.

We only send you emails if you ticked the box giving permission, as required by Canada's anti-spam law (CASL). You can unsubscribe at any time. Forms are hosted by a form provider (such as Tally or Google Forms), which stores the answers on its servers. These may be outside Canada.

If you arrived through a team member's invite link, the link's name tag (like ?ref=anna) is saved on your device and added to the application form, so we know who invited you. It doesn't contain anything else.

5. How long we keep it

We keep form information while you're on the Founding Team and for up to 12 months after you leave, then delete it, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email us and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
