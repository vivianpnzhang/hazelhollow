Not financial advice

HazelHollow is a budgeting tool. It helps you split the money you already have across the weeks until your next money arrives. That's all it does.

  • It does not give investment, tax, credit, debt or lending advice.
  • It does not hold, move or lend anyone's money, and it isn't a bank.
  • Its weekly number is an estimate based only on what you type in. Always check your real bank balance before big decisions.
  • Suggested costs (like textbook budgets) come from published university estimates. Your real costs may be different, and you can change every number.

Where to get real help

  • Your school's financial aid / student awards office, for OSAP, bursaries and emergency funds.
  • The Financial Consumer Agency of Canada, for free, neutral money information from the Government of Canada.
  • A licensed financial professional, for personal advice.
