Volunteer roles

Join the Founding Team

HazelHollow is a free budgeting app built by a student. We're looking for a small group of students to test it, share it and help make it better.

This is an honest, volunteer role on a student-built project. It's unpaid, it's not a job or an internship, and it takes 30 minutes a week at most. You can stop any time.

The two roles

RoleWhat you doTimeLinkedIn title
Founding TesterUse the app, do 1–2 small weekly "missions", and fill out a 5-question feedback form.15–30 min/weekFounding Tester, HazelHollow
Campus AmbassadorShare 1 post a month, invite friends with your personal link, and pass on feedback you hear.10–20 min/weekCampus Ambassador, HazelHollow

What you get

  • Free HazelHollow Pro while you're active.
  • A Founding Team badge in the app forever: a tiny golden hazelnut.
  • Your name on the Founding Team wall, if you'd like it there.
  • A downloadable Founding Team card.
  • A LinkedIn recommendation from the founder after a full term of real participation.

How it works

  1. 1

    Apply

    Fill out a short form. It takes about 2 minutes.

  2. 2

    Get your code

    You'll get a personal team code. Type it into the app to open Pro and the Tester Hub.

  3. 3

    Do weekly missions

    Small tasks each week, like "try the roommate split", plus quick feedback.

Two promises we ask you to keep

  • Always say you're connected. You get free Pro, so when you post about HazelHollow, keep the disclosure line (like "#FoundingTester, I get free Pro for testing HazelHollow"). Canada's Competition Bureau expects this, and it's just honest.
  • Only share your honest opinion. Never post fake reviews or say things you don't believe.
