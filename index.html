The idea

Why a squirrel?

Every fall, squirrels gather hazelnuts and tuck them into a cozy tree hollow so they're okay all winter. Students live the same way: money comes in big chunks (OSAP, a summer job, help from family) and has to last until the next harvest.

How it works

Three steps, one calm number

  1. 1

    Fill your hollow

    Tell HazelHollow what money you have now, when your next money comes in, and your fixed bills. It takes under a minute.

  2. 2

    Get one number

    See how much you can spend this week so your stash lasts until the next harvest. Tap it to see the math.

  3. 3

    Log in 5 seconds

    Tap Coffee, Groceries or Takeout, type the amount, done. Your weekly number updates right away.
